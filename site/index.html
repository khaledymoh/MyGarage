MyGarage

Every service, upgrade and wash. One garage.

Track what you've done to your cars, see exactly what you've spent, and get reminded when a service is due — by time, no odometer needed.

Screens

Your car at a glance

Features

Built for people who care about their cars

Maintenance, upgrades, detailing

Three record types kept apart, each with items split into parts and labour, notes and a receipt photo.

Know what you've spent

Total spent all time, by type and by month, plus your upgrade build list ranked by cost.

Reminders by time

Oil every 6 months, inspection every 12 — set your own intervals and see what's due soon or overdue.

Search and filters

Find any record for the car you picked by service, date or mileage.

Home-screen widget

Total spent and upcoming services on your home screen, in three sizes.

Synced and private

Sign in with Google; your garage is backed up to your account and only you can read it.

Install

Three steps

  1. Download the APKTap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Allow the installIf asked, allow your browser to install apps (Android shows the switch for you).
  3. Open and sign inOpen the downloaded file, install, then sign in with Google.

Updates install over the existing app and keep your data. The app tells you when a new version is out.

Download stuck at 100% in Chrome? Chrome sometimes holds new APK files for a safety check. Cancel it and try again, or download with another browser such as Samsung Internet or Firefox. You can also get the APK from the GitHub release page.
